## Summary
`IEntitlementService` enforces, in code:
- the daily Submitted limit
- the daily scoring cap
- the per-tenant managed-inference spend ceiling

Spend is metered in an append-only `SpendLedger`. Its only exception is anonymisation by the privileged account-deletion job (F5.3, Decision D9, 2026-11-22). Phase 5 adds the plan-specific values.

## Scope
**In:**
- `IEntitlementService` (`PlanEntitlementService`)
- `UsageCounter` (Submitted per day)
- Scoring cap of 50 per day
- `SpendLedger` metering every model call (tokens and cost)
- Monthly spend ceiling per tenant
- `GET /usage`
- Dashboard meter

**Out:**
- Plan tiers and their values (F5.1)
- BYOK (F5.2)

## Business rules
1. **Quota unit:** one `Submitted` outcome. Prepared, queued, skipped, handed-off, cancelled and failed applications do not count.
2. The daily counter resets at 00:00 in the user's timezone.
3. Until Phase 5 sets the plan values, every tenant gets 1 Submitted per day and a managed spend ceiling of $3/month on the operator's key. BYOK only arrives in F5.2.
4. **Limit reached mid-cycle (E7):** the current submit finishes; later submits wait for the reset. Sourcing, scoring and tailoring continue.
5. **Ceiling reached mid-cycle (E8):** the model call in flight finishes, and no new model calls happen this month. Items already prepared can still be submitted.
6. Checks are server-side, immediately before each submit and each model call. They are never in the client or in prompts.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a daily limit of 1 and 1 Submitted today, then a second approval is held until the reset and the UI says so.
2. Given a spend ceiling reached, then no further model calls are made (test with a fake provider) and the dashboard shows "AI budget reached".
3. Given usage reaches 80% of the ceiling, then a notification is sent once.
4. Given `GET /usage`, then it returns the remaining daily submits, the scoring cap left, and the spend used and ceiling.
