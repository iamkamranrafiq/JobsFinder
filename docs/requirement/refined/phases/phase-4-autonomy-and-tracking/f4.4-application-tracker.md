## Summary
A table and kanban view of the user's applications by **agent-side** state, with a timeline per application. There is no tracking after submission (no viewed / interview / offer / rejected).

## Scope
**In:**
- `GET /applications`, `GET /applications/{id}`
- Tracker columns: Prepared, Queued, Submitted, Needs you, Failed
- Timeline from `ApplicationEvent`
- Filters by state, date and channel
- Cursor pagination

**Out:**
- Employer responses or interview tracking (removed, Q5)

## Business rules
1. Only the five tracker states are shown. Skipped, expired and cancelled items are hidden from the tracker and stay in the audit history. A held auto-submit (`AutoSubmitPending`, F4.2) shows under **Queued**, with its cancel countdown. *(Ruling 2026-11-22)*
2. Statuses read exactly as they are. "Submitted" appears only for confirmed submissions.
3. A submitted application shows its confirmation evidence (screenshot or provider message id).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given applications in each state, then each one appears in the right column.
2. Given a Submitted application, then its timeline shows prepared -> (queued / auto-pending) -> submitted with timestamps, and the confirmation evidence.
3. Given 500 applications, then the list pages with a cursor and each page loads < 300 ms p95.
