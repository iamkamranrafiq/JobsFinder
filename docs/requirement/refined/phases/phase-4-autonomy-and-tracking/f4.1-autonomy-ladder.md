## Summary
Users set how much the agent may do without approval: one global level, with overrides per channel and per employer. The default is DryRun, and the level only rises when the user raises it.

## Scope
**In:**
- Levels:
  1. DryRun
  2. Review-first
  3. Auto-apply (scoped)
  4. Auto-apply (broad)
- `GET/PUT /settings/autonomy`
- Per-channel (email / web form) and per-employer overrides
- Whitelists: employers, email addresses, trusted ATS
- A user-set fit threshold for auto-apply

**Out:**
- Plan gating of levels 3 and 4 (F5.1). Until Phase 5, every user can choose any level.

## Business rules
1. New users start at **DryRun**. When Phase 4 ships, every existing account is also set to DryRun. *(Ruling 2026-11-22)*
2. **Scoped auto-apply** submits automatically only to whitelisted employers or channels, at or above the user's threshold. Everything else goes to review. The employer whitelist is the **same list** as the "pre-approved employers" used for motivation answers in F3.2. *(Ruling 2026-11-22)*
3. **Broad auto-apply** submits automatically to any `AtsUrl` or `Email` posting at or above the threshold, within the daily limits.
4. Overrides win in this order: employer > channel > global.
5. Lowering the level returns pending auto-submits to the review queue immediately (E12).
6. Hard stops, the honesty invariant and the daily caps apply at every level.
7. Every non-production environment is forced to DryRun, whatever the user setting (F1.7).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a new account, then the level is DryRun and nothing is ever submitted.
2. Given scoped auto-apply with employer X whitelisted and threshold 80, then a score-85 role at X goes to the cancel window, and a score-85 role at Y goes to review.
3. Given a per-employer override "Review-first" for X under a global "Broad", then X's roles go to review.
4. Given I lower the level from Broad to Review-first, then all pending auto-submits move to the queue within 5 s.
