## Summary
Four plans with daily Submitted limits, feature gating and managed-inference spend ceilings. Prices are indicative until pricing is defined. Payments are a separate pending feature (F5.6).

## Scope
**In:**
- Plan entity and assignment
- Daily limits per plan
- Spend ceilings
- Feature gates:
  - Free: review-first only
  - Basic: + all sources, saved searches, tracker, digests
  - Standard: + scoped auto-apply
  - Premium: + broad auto-apply
- Upgrade prompts
- Plan change takes effect immediately

**Out:**
- Charging money (F5.6, pending)

## Business rules

| Plan | Submitted / day | Inference | Managed spend ceiling / month | Price (indicative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | 1 | BYOK only | — | $0 |
| Basic | 5 | Managed or BYOK | $3 | ~$6 |
| Standard | 15 | Managed or BYOK | $7 | ~$14 |
| Premium | fair use, cap 40 | Managed or BYOK | $15 | ~$29 |

1. Free is **BYOK-only** for now (OQ2). This is revisited in Pending.
2. **Downgrade mid-day (E22):** nothing already Submitted is cancelled. Further submits wait until the next reset.
3. Selecting a level above the plan's gate is refused with an upgrade prompt.
4. Until payments exist, plans are assigned by the operator, for example to early users.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a Free user, then the auto-apply levels are disabled with an upgrade prompt, and only BYOK inference is available.
2. Given a Basic user with 5 Submitted today, then a 6th waits until the reset.
3. Given a Premium user, then the daily cap is 40 Submitted.
4. Given the operator changes a user's plan, then the new limits apply immediately.

## Decisions (2026-11-22)
- **D2 - Prices:** kept out of every Story and every UI surface until pricing is defined (F5.6).
- **D3 - Upgrade prompt:** until payments exist, it shows "Request access" (the operator assigns the plan, BR4). There is no payment link and no price.
- **D4 - Ungated features:** the source, saved-search, tracker and digest gates have no AC yet. They stay inside the plan-gating Story (JF-139), and ACs must be added before building.
- **D5 - Plans at launch:** new sign-ups get Free. Existing and early users are assigned Basic by the operator.
- **D6 - Downgrade and autonomy:** a downgrade that removes an autonomy level is treated as lowering autonomy (F4.1-BR5). Pending auto-submits return to the review queue.
- **D10 - Operator role:** operator plan assignment needs a minimal operator role with MFA. F1.1 does not define one yet, so it must be added before JF-137 ships.
- **Known conflict (pricing, pending):** at USD 0.10 per application, the $3 / $7 / $15 ceilings run out long before the 5 / 15 / 40 daily limits are reached. They are revisited together with pricing (F5.6).
