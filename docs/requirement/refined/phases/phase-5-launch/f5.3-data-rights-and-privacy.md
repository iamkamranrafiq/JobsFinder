## Summary
Users can export all their data and delete their account. Operator access to user data is break-glass only, with the user's consent. Consent and data-processing notices are shown in the product.

## Scope
**In:**
- `POST /account/export` (asynchronous, downloadable archive)
- `DELETE /account`
- Break-glass access flow
- Data-processing notice
- Consent records (mailbox, diversity answers)

**Out:**
- Jurisdiction-specific compliance work (pending, refined spec section 6)

## Business rules
1. **Export** contains:
   - the profile and its versions
   - the raw CV
   - diversity answers
   - saved searches, blocklists
   - applications and their artifacts
   - the audit log for the user

   It is delivered as a ZIP behind a signed URL that expires after 24 h.
2. **Delete** (E14):
   - cancels pending submits immediately
   - revokes the mailbox and BYOK credentials
   - deletes all tenant data within 30 days
   - anonymises `SpendLedger` rows instead of deleting them
3. **Break-glass:** the user grants consent in-app. Access lasts ≤ 24 h, is limited to that tenant, and every access is audit-logged.
4. The data-processing notice says that job descriptions and CV text are sent to the AI provider, and that diversity answers never are.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given an export request, then within 15 min a download link is available and the archive contains every listed item.
2. Given account deletion, then I can no longer sign in, pending auto-submits are cancelled, and after 30 days no tenant rows remain except anonymised spend rows.
3. Given an operator without the user's consent, then access to that tenant's data is refused.

## Decisions (2026-11-22)
- **D9 - Deletion vs append-only records:** the audit log (F1.5) and `SpendLedger` (F4.6) are append-only. On account deletion, a documented privileged job, not the app's database role, anonymises the tenant's audit and spend rows. This is the only permitted exception to append-only, and every run is itself audit-logged.
- **D10 - Operator role:** break-glass access needs a minimal operator role with MFA. F1.1 does not define one yet, so it must be added before JF-146 is built.
- **D11 - Notice and consent records:** JF-147 has no acceptance criteria yet and needs them before it is built.
- **Account deletion and auto-submits:** deletion cancels held auto-submits immediately, and the F4.2 release checks treat a deleted account as a failed check.
