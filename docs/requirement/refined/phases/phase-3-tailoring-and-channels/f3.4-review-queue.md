## Summary
The product's control surface. Each prepared application is shown with everything needed to decide, and the user can approve, edit then approve, skip, or block the employer. It works on a phone.

## User stories
- As a user, I approve a well-matched application in under 2 minutes.
- As a user, I edit the tailored CV or message before it goes out.
- As a user, I block an employer straight from the queue.

## Scope
**In:**
- `GET /applications/queue`
- `POST /applications/{id}/approve`, `/edit`, `/skip`
- Block employer
- Queue UI (mobile-responsive, keyboard-navigable)
- Expiry job
- Idempotency keys on approve

## Business rules
1. Each item shows:
   - the role, fit score and reasoning
   - the tailored-CV diff
   - the drafted message or a screenshot of the filled form
   - the evidence behind each screening answer
2. Approval is idempotent: a repeated approve with the same key has no further effect.
3. Items expire after **7 days** without action and leave the queue (E6).
4. Approval is refused when:
   - the posting is `Closed` (E4) -> the item shows "Posting closed"
   - the item was prepared from an older profile version (E5) -> it must be re-prepared
5. Skipped and expired items leave the queue and are kept in the audit history.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given a queued item, then all the content in rule 1 is visible on desktop and on a 375 px-wide screen.
2. Given approve clicked twice, then only one submission happens.
3. Given an item 7 days old, then it disappears from the queue and an audit entry records the expiry.
4. Given the posting closed after preparation, then approve is refused with "Posting closed".
5. Given I updated my CV after the item was prepared, then approve prompts me to re-prepare it.
6. Given I choose "Block employer", then the company is added to my blocklist and its other queued items are removed.
