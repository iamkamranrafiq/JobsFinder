## Summary
Once a day, each user gets an email and an in-app digest of new roles at or above their fit threshold.

## Scope
**In:**
- Digest generation after each user's daily cycles
- Email via the platform's transactional email provider
- In-app digest list
- Unsubscribe and frequency setting (daily / off)
- Password-reset emails, using the same email provider

## Business rules
1. The digest includes roles scored ≥ the threshold since the last digest, sorted by score, showing at most 10 with a link to see all.
2. Nothing is sent when there are no new high-fit roles.
3. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.
4. The email contains no CV content: only the role, company, score and a link.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Phase 2 exit:** given a user with a saved search, then each day a digest of their high-fit roles arrives (email and in-app).
2. Given no new roles ≥ the threshold, then no email is sent.
3. Given I unsubscribe, then no further digest emails are sent, and the in-app digest still works.

## Dependencies

## Open points
